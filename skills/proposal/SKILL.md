# Writing a proposal

Use this for any request that ends in a document a client says yes or no to: a proposal, a quote, a scope, a pricing page for one client.

## Before you write
1. Find the client in the notes you were given. If they are not there, say so at the top and write for the person described in the request.
2. Take the packages and prices from the **offer-ladder note in your COMPANY NOTES** — it is already in this prompt, in full. Do not go looking for it in Notion, Drive or anywhere else, and never say you could not reach it. Never invent a price. Only if the ladder genuinely does not cover what was asked, write "(price to confirm)" and say which line is missing.
3. Write in the voice set out in the voice note in your COMPANY NOTES.

## The shape
Follow `template.md` beside this file, section for section. Keep the headings. Do not add sections.

## Rules
- Three options, always. The middle one is the one we want them to pick; say so in one line under the table.
- Price per month, ex GST, and say so once under the table.
- One page. If it will not fit, cut the background, not the scope.
- No discounts. A cheaper option is a smaller scope, never the same scope for less.
- The last line is the next step and a date, never "let me know".
