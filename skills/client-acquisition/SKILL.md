# Winning clients

Use this for any task about finding, attracting or converting a client we do not have yet:
prospecting, outreach, content, referrals, re-engagement, partnerships, paid.

Who we are for, what we sell and for how much are in the **icp**, **services** and **offer-ladder**
notes in your COMPANY NOTES — already in this prompt, in full. Read them there; do not go looking
for them in Notion, Drive or any other tool. Targets and current numbers come from the
acquisition-plan note if you were given it.

## The six channels

1. **Outbound** — build a list against the ICP, enrich it, write one first touch per prospect.
2. **Inbound** — content, newsletter and search: publish for the problem the ICP already has.
3. **Referral** — clients at NPS 9+, past clients, and people who said no for timing reasons.
4. **Re-engagement** — deals lost or gone quiet over 60 days old.
5. **Partnerships** — people who serve the same ICP without competing with us.
6. **Paid** — only against an offer that has already converted organically.

## Every prospect becomes a note

The moment a prospect is real, it gets a note under `Agents Office/Clients/` following the
`client-lifecycle` skill, at `Stage: Prospect`, with one extra header line:

```
Source: outbound | inbound | referral | re-engagement | partnership | paid
```

Source is how we learn which channel works. Never leave it blank, never guess it.

## Rules
- **One personal reason per outreach.** Every first touch names something real and specific about
  that prospect — something you actually found, quoted, with where you found it. No template
  sent to a list.
- **Never invent proof.** Results, client names, case studies and numbers come from the win log or
  a customer note. If we do not have the proof, do not imply it.
- **Never invent a price.** Use the offer-ladder note in your COMPANY NOTES; only if it does not
  cover what was asked, write "(price to confirm)".
- **Nothing sends, posts, or spends without the owner's OK** — build the list, write the message,
  prepare the campaign, then stop. An approved routine sends; you do not.
- **No volume for its own sake.** Twenty researched prospects beat two hundred scraped ones; the
  plan's weekly target is a ceiling, not a quota to fill with noise.
- **Respect a no.** A prospect who declined is not re-approached inside six months, and never on
  the same offer. Anyone who asked not to be contacted is marked `Churned` with the reason and
  never contacted again.
- Say which channel you worked and what it produced, so the weekly acquisition report can count
  it without guessing.
