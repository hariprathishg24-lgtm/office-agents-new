# Flagging a risk

Use this for any task that finds something that could hurt the agency: a contract clause, a
client too large to lose, a late payment, a lapsed vendor, a connector outside policy.

## Where it goes

One register: `Agents Office/Risk Register.md`. One line per open risk, newest first, under
`## Open`. A risk that is closed moves to `## Closed` with the date and what resolved it —
it is never deleted.

```
- [HIGH] 2026-09-15 · Concentration · Harbourside is 34% of monthly revenue · owner: conc · next: model the loss case
```

Severity is `HIGH` (could end the agency or a client relationship), `MEDIUM` (costs real money
or time), `LOW` (worth knowing, not worth acting on yet).

## Before you write
1. Read the register. **If the risk is already open, update that line — never add a second.**
2. Check the number against its source: `80-Finance` for money, the client note in
   `Agents Office/Clients/` for a client, `10-Business` for contracts and vendors.
3. Say what the next step is and who it belongs to — a risk with no next step is a complaint.

## Rules
- Name the risk in one sentence a non-specialist understands. No jargon, no hedging.
- Every HIGH is repeated at the top of your deliverable so the owner sees it without opening the
  register.
- Quote the number and where it came from. Never estimate one.
- **You flag. You never act**: you do not pause a client, cancel a vendor, hold a payment or
  change a contract. Name who should decide, and what you recommend they decide.
- Do not re-flag something already in `## Closed` unless it has genuinely recurred — then it is a
  new line that says "recurrence".
